Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Line, One Laugh: The Office Joke Open](https://make.fun/bounty/u-myronera-one-line-one-laugh-the-office-joke-open-fe8204d4): one original one-line joke about office life, at most 200 characters.

> My boss told me to take ownership, so I put my name on the bug — now it's mine forever, like a timeshare.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes the joke in `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt` byte-for-byte, after validating the sealed scorer's gate (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only white space), and verifies the output by reading it back.
